How landmark positions translate into operational triggers.
Closed Fist (Rock)
All 4 finger PIP joints folded towards palm. Thumb resting on lateral side or folded.
Triggers: Rock in RPS Arena.
Open Hand (Paper)
All 4 or 5 finger landmarks fully extended away from wrist.
Triggers: Paper in RPS Arena • 5 Count.
Peace Sign (Scissors)
Index & Middle fingertips extended vertically. Ring and Pinky folded.
Triggers: Scissors in RPS Arena.
Precision Index Point
Landmark 8 extended. Other fingers folded.
Triggers: Steering snake head • Air keyboard cursor.
